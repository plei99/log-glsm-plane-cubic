# Convex complete intersections beyond the previous ambient catalogue

The new sample adds eleven presentations to the existing forty-three. Curve classes are bounded by the sum of coefficients in the specified effective Mori basis, at most two. This is an explicit truncation, not a finiteness assertion based on the defining bundle's degree: several summands are nef but not ample and have degree zero on nonzero effective curves.

## Non-Fano toric ambients

Write F_e = P(O ⊕ O(e)) with negative section S and ruling fiber F. Then
\[
S^2=-e,\quad S F=1,\quad F^2=0,\quad -K_{F_e}=2S+(e+2)F.
\]
A fan has rays (0,1), (1,0), (0,-1), (-1,e), with the cones spanned by consecutive rays in cyclic order. Product fans give the higher-dimensional ambients below. Since (-K)·S=2-e, F_2 is weak Fano but not Fano, while F_3 has non-nef anticanonical divisor. Their products remain non-Fano.

A divisor aS+bF is nef precisely when a≥0 and b≥ea. On a smooth projective toric variety nef Cartier divisors are globally generated. Pullbacks to genus-zero trees have nonnegative degree on every component, so their H¹ vanishes. Thus sums of the line bundles below are convex in the usual **genus-zero** sense; no higher-genus R¹ term is discarded. See [Cox–Little–Schenck, Toric Varieties](https://dacox.people.amherst.edu/toric.html), chapters 3 and 6, for the fan, divisor, and nef-cone framework. The displayed intersection formulas also verify the nef inequalities directly.

A general member of |S+eF| is a smooth rational section disjoint from S. This proves the product descriptions of NF1, NF4, NF5 even though their residual anticanonical classes need not be ample on the whole ambient space. The restriction to the complete intersection is ample. For NF2 and NF3, nef anticanonical systems are basepoint free, and a general member is smooth. Adjunction gives trivial canonical class. The exact sequence 0→K_X→O_X→O_Y→0, Serre duality, and H^i(O_X)=0 for i>0 on these rational toric ambients give connectedness and the required intermediate O-cohomology vanishing. Thus NF2 is K3 and NF3 is CY3.

The polarization used for marked H insertions is S+(e+1)F+H. It pairs with a Mori tuple (d_S,d_F,d_H) as d_S+d_F+d_H.

For NF1, NF4 and NF5, the rational section itself has ambient curve class S+eF, of Mori-coefficient sum e+1. Its nonconstant covers therefore lie outside the degree-two cutoff. Positive compact curves inside this cutoff come from the projective factor. Ambient maps on the negative section are still included in the twisted calculation, even when their curve class is not represented inside the complete intersection.

## A nonisolated curve family in NF3

On the divisor S×P², the anticanonical equation restricts to a cubic equation independent of the S coordinate, because (2S+4F)·S=0. For a general hypersurface,
\[
D=Y\cap(S\times\mathbf P^2)=\mathbf P^1\times E,
\]
where E is a smooth plane cubic. The normal bundle of D in Y is the pullback of O_{P¹}(−2). For a fiber C=P¹×{p}, the normal exact sequence is
\[
0\to O_C\to N_{C/Y}\to O_C(-2)\to0.
\]
It splits because H¹(P¹,O(2))=0. Hence N_{C/Y}=O⊕O(−2), and these fibers move in an elliptic family. The isolated (−1,−1)-curve formula must not be applied to them. Their ordinary positive-degree invariants remain unknown in the output unless separately proved; computed ambient-twist zeros are not substituted for them.

## Type-A partial flags

For X=Fl(k₁,…,k_s;N), H_i=c₁(det S_{k_i}^∨). Put k₀=0, k_{s+1}=N and block sizes b_i=k_{i+1}−k_i. Then
\[
\dim X=\sum_{i<j}b_i b_j,
\qquad -K_X=\sum_{i=0}^{s-1}(b_i+b_{i+1})H_{i+1}.
\]
Each H_i is globally generated by the Plücker projection. All nonnegative combinations used here are therefore convex. The positive residual classes in the Fano cases are ample; the CY cases are sums of ample defining divisors with total class −K_X.

The flag varieties have respectively 6,12,12 torus fixed points and 9,30,30 invariant curves. Fl(1,2;3) is the point-line incidence hypersurface in P²×(P²)^∨. Fl(1,3;4) is the point-hyperplane incidence hypersurface in P³×(P³)^∨. These give independent product-projective Chern computations for PF1, PF2, PF5, PF6. Fl(1,2;4) parametrizes a line inside a two-plane; it is a P¹-bundle over Gr(2,4) and a P²-bundle over P³.

For the GKM model, a fixed point is an ordered partition of the coordinate indices into the blocks. Tangent weights are λ_j−λ_i for an index i in an earlier block than j. An invariant curve swaps such a pair between blocks A<B; its Mori degree has ones in positions A,…,B−1 and zeros elsewhere. See [Anderson–Fulton, Equivariant Cohomology in Algebraic Geometry](https://people.math.osu.edu/anderson.2804/ecag/bookECAG.2021.8.31.pdf), chapter 10 §3 and the general G/P invariant-curve proposition on printed p.296.

The code represents the tangent restriction along this curve by O(2) and normal summands of degrees zero or one. For a tangent root (i,j) other than the edge (a,b), the normal degree is one exactly when i=a and block(j)≤B, or j=b and block(i)≥A. It is zero otherwise. The corresponding starting character is λ_j−λ_i. The test compares the ending characters ell−degree·w with the full tangent representation at the other endpoint, including the reversed tangent −w. This verifies the equivariant K-class, which determines the virtual RΓ factor used by localization. It also checks the total degree against c₁(TX)·β. For F_e the degrees −e on the negative section are retained, giving the required H¹ numerator factors.

## Thirty-three isolated fibers in PF4

Let B=Gr(2,4), S its tautological rank-two bundle, H=c₁(S^∨), and X=P_B(S)=Fl(1,2;4). The two defining equations restrict to linear forms on each P¹ fiber. Their coefficients are a section of
\[
W=(S^\vee\otimes O_B(1))\oplus(S^\vee\otimes O_B(2)).
\]
This rank-four bundle is globally generated on the fourfold B. The general coefficient section is transverse, so its zero scheme is reduced and consists precisely of the fibers contained in Y.

If x,y are the Chern roots of S^∨ and H=x+y, then
\[
c_4(W)=(x+H)(y+H)(x+2H)(y+2H)
=(xy)^2+8xyH^2+12H^4.
\]
Using ∫(xy)²=1, ∫xyH²=1, ∫H⁴=2 on Gr(2,4) gives
\[
\boxed{\#\{\text{contained fibers}\}=1+8+24=33.}
\]

For any such fiber C, N_{C/X}=O_C^4 and the defining bundle restricts to O_C(1)^2. Transversality of the coefficient section makes the induced map on H⁰ an isomorphism. Therefore the normal kernel has rank two, degree −2, and no H⁰; its splitting is O(−1)⊕O(−1). The fibers are disjoint. Any stable map of degree (k,0) is constant under the projection to B, because the Plücker class on B is ample. Its image is therefore one of exactly these 33 fibers. This excludes extra irreducible images and intersections between distinct fibers in every pure fiber degree.

The local rational-curve formula of [Faber–Pandharipande, Hodge integrals and Gromov–Witten theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9810173), Theorem 3, now gives
\[
\boxed{N_{g,(k,0)}=33\,\frac{|B_{2g}|}{2g(2g-2)!}\,k^{2g-3}\qquad(g\ge2,\ k\ge1).}
\]
In particular N_{2,(1,0)}=11/80, N_{3,(1,0)}=11/2016, N_{2,(2,0)}=11/40, and N_{3,(2,0)}=11/252. The implementation independently checks genus-zero localization on two rays: N_{0,(1,0)}=33 and N_{0,(2,0)}=33/8. The geometric proof above, rather than genus-zero values alone, justifies the higher-genus conclusion.

## The other projection: 88 lines and five conics

The projection X→P³ remembers the line L. Write h=c₁(L^∨), Q=C⁴/L, and xi for O_{P(Q)}(1). Then H₁=h and H₂=h+xi. The two equations have relative degrees one and two, with classes xi+2h and 2xi+3h.

The linear equation is identically zero on a P² fiber exactly at the zeros of a section of Q^∨⊗O(2). Since c(Q^∨)=1−h+h²−h³,
\[
\int_{\mathbf P^3}c_3(Q^\vee\otimes O(2))=-1+2-4+8=5.
\]
For general equations these five fibers contain smooth conics. Away from them, the first equation defines a line in the P² fiber; this whole line belongs to Y precisely when the quadratic equation restricts to zero.

Lines in the P² fibers are parametrized by Fl(1,3;4), recording L inside a three-plane V. Put K=V/L. Containment is the zero condition for the globally generated rank-five bundle
\[
(K^\vee\otimes O(2h))\oplus(\operatorname{Sym}^2 K^\vee\otimes O(3h))
\]
on this fivefold. Fixed-point integration of its top Chern class gives **88**. Explicitly, at a fixed flag with coordinate weights a for L and b,c for K, the numerator is
\[
(-b-2a)(-c-2a)(-2b-3a)(-b-c-3a)(-2c-3a).
\]
The denominator is the product of the five tangent weights of Fl(1,3;4). Summing over its twelve fixed flags gives 88. Global generation follows from restricting the original equations: linear and quadratic forms on each P² fiber restrict surjectively to a line. Thus a general pair gives a reduced zero scheme. At the five exceptional base points the quadratic is a smooth irreducible conic, so none of these 88 lines lies there.

The fibers are isolated and mutually disjoint. For a line, the nonzero linear equation eliminates its O(1) normal direction in P²; transversality of the three quadratic coefficients gives H⁰(N_{C/Y})=0. For a conic, the quadratic equation eliminates its O(4) normal direction in P², while transversality of the three coefficients of the linear equation gives an isomorphism H⁰(O³)→H⁰(O(2)). In both cases adjunction and splitting on P¹ give N_{C/Y}=O(−1)².

Every map of class (0,k) lies in one of these fibers, because H₁ is pulled back from an ample divisor on P³. Consequently, for g≥2,
\[
N_{g,(0,k)}=\frac{|B_{2g}|}{2g(2g-2)!}
\left(88k^{2g-3}+\mathbf1_{2\mid k}\,5(k/2)^{2g-3}\right).
\]
Independent genus-zero localization gives 88 and 16 in degrees (0,1) and (0,2); the latter equals 5+88/8.

## Mixed degree (1,1): 634 isolated curves

An irreducible map of bidegree (1,1) projects isomorphically to a projective line under both the P³ and Plücker projections. It is an embedded rational curve. The only possible nonconstant reducible genus-zero type has degrees (1,0) and (0,1). Its two components project to the two axes in P¹×P¹.

The convex ambient moduli Mbar_(0,0)(X,(1,1)) has virtual and actual dimension dim(X)−3+c₁(TX)·(1,1)=7, and the bundle of sections of the defining equations has rank (2+1)+(3+1)=7. This section bundle is globally generated: restrictions of product polynomials of bidegrees (1,1) and (1,2) generate O(2) and O(3) on the diagonal P¹. They also generate the respective spaces of sections on the two-axis union, by the restriction sequence with kernel O(a−1,b−1) on P¹×P¹. The nodal locus has dimension six, so a general pair of equations has no zero on it. In particular, a (1,0) fiber line and a (0,1) fiber line do not meet inside general Y.

The zero scheme on the smooth irreducible-map locus is therefore reduced and zero-dimensional. Convex genus-zero localization, independently on bases four and five, gives **634**. Each curve is embedded and isolated; H⁰(N)=0 and deg N=−2 imply N=O(−1)². A higher-genus map of this bidegree has one of these rational images, because each degree-one projection forces rational normalization; the only alternative reducible image was excluded above. Thus
\[
N_{g,(1,1)}=634\,\frac{|B_{2g}|}{2g(2g-2)!}.
\]
This isolation and boundary argument is required; the genus-zero number by itself would not justify this formula.

## Relation scope and comparison conventions

The backend continues to use the full derived dual-Euler twist, with one common t and with all higher-genus R¹ contributions. No higher-genus quantum Lefschetz replacement is made. The CJR sign is (−1)^{rank(E)(1−g)+c₁(E)·β}. Writing L=Z+B for the full equivariant pushforward, its negative t-coefficients vanish and its constant coefficient is sigma G; positive t-coefficients are not determined by this compact comparison. The marked comparison conventions are those documented in [GENUS_FOUR_MARKED_SOURCES.md, §4](GENUS_FOUR_MARKED_SOURCES.md), from the supplied CJR III draft, Theorem 8.4, Remark 8.5 and §9.4. The nonample summands do not justify discarding classes of zero E-degree or claiming a finite all-degree search.

Every claimed tautological relation is tested by exact membership in an explicit Pixton matrix. These rows are ambient-regularity and symmetry relations and their declared tautological consequences. This driver does not compute or eliminate individual positive-dimensional infinity cycles; it infers only the specified numerical infinity aggregates. [Janda's semisimple-CohFT comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.03419) gives a theoretical reason that many reconstruction relations are already Pixton consequences, but it is not used as a blanket theorem about every specialization in this program. Exact certificates remain the criterion for the specific rows computed here.
